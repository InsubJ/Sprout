# Monument Forest & Poetic Summaries

## Overview

The Monument Forest honors user persistence. When habits complete, the system generates a poetic reflection summary encapsulating the user's journey, streak history, and resilience.

## Poetic Reflection Attributes

- `poetic_summary`: Text summary generated upon habit completion (e.g. "Twelve quiet returns turned scattered minutes into a reading ritual.").
- **Variant Preservation**: The visual appearance of the completed plant preserves its historical growth variant (`flawless`, `steady`, or `scarred`).
