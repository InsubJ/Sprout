# Species Discovery Grid & Botanical Catalog

## Overview

The Botanical Lab (`LabScreen`) provides a compendium of all 20 native plant species.

## Discovered vs. Locked States

- **Discovered Species**: Rendered in full color with interactive growth simulation controls.
- **Locked Species**: Rendered in silhouette / monochrome with message "Not discovered yet. Grow and complete this species in Sprout to unlock."
