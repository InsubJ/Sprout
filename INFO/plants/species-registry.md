# Species Registry & Plant Types

## Overview

All plant species supported in Sprout are registered in `nativePlantRegistry` in `apps/mobile/src/features/plants/plantRegistry.ts`.

## Plant Species List

- `bonsai`: Classic Miniature Bonsai Tree (Tier: Common).
- `pothos`: Trailing Golden Pothos (Tier: Common).
- `spider_plant`: Tufted Spider Plant (Tier: Common).
- `lavender`: French Lavender Bloom (Tier: Uncommon).
- `sunflower`: Golden Sunflower (Tier: Uncommon).
- `poinsettia`: Holiday Poinsettia (Tier: Uncommon).
- `string_of_pearls`: Succulent String of Pearls (Tier: Rare).
- `waratah`: Crimson Waratah Flower (Tier: Rare).
- `remy`: Remy Humanoid Plant (Tier: Rare).
- `sprig`: Sprig Dog Plant (Tier: Mythical).
- `jason`: Jason Character Plant (Tier: Mythical).
- `blossom`: Blossom Flora (Tier: Mythical).
- `alocasia_tiny_dancer`: Alocasia Tiny Dancer (Tier: Rare).
- `begonia_maculata`: Polka Dot Begonia (Tier: Rare).
- `desert_cactus`: Desert Cactus (Tier: Common).
- `ethereal_sakura`: Ethereal Cherry Blossom (Tier: Mythical).
- `golden_oak`: Golden Oak Tree (Tier: Rare).
- `maranta`: Prayer Plant (Tier: Uncommon).
- `midnight_rose`: Midnight Rose (Tier: Mythical).
- `phalaenopsis_orchid`: Phalaenopsis Orchid (Tier: Rare).
