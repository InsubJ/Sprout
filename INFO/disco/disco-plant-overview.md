# Disco Plant Overview

## Overview

The Disco Plant (`🪩 Disco Plant`) is a unique mythical habit plant available to all gardeners. Unlike standard habits that require manual routine check-ins, the Disco Plant is watered through ad views or donations, spreading energy and granting custom plant generation credits.

## Key Features

- **Special Status Badges**: Displays dynamic states (`🎉 Dancing!`, `😄 Happy`, `🍂 Wilting`).
- **Reward Credit Award**: Completing the Disco Plant habit progression unlocks +1 AI custom plant generation credit.
- **Plant God Transformation**: When AI custom plant generation is unlocked, the Disco Plant card transforms into the glowing **✨ Plant God** card.
