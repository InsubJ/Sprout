# Disco Habit Card & Status Badge Components

## Overview

The `DiscoHabitCard` displays the Disco Plant (or Plant God when active) inside the main garden carousel.

## Sub-Components

- **`DiscoStatusBadge`**: Pill badge showing state metadata (`🎉 Dancing!`, `😄 Happy`, `🍂 Wilting`).
- **`RewardProgress`**: Progress bar showing progress toward earning the next AI custom plant credit.
- **`PlantGod`**: Celestial animated figure rendered when Plant God mode is active.
